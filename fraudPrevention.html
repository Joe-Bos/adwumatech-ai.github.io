Project Overview

Safer Financial Systems Through Responsible Data Collection

Better identity checks. Stronger fraud prevention.

We collect limited, consent-based data to improve KYC and fraud models for financial services. This helps teams:

  • Verify real customers faster
  • Stop identity theft and account takeover
  • Lower fraud losses and false alarms
  • Meet regulatory requirements

Why We Collect

Why ID and Face Checks Matter

Fraud now includes deepfakes and synthetic IDs. Real-world signals help models separate real users from attacks.

What We Collect

Only data needed for identity checks and fraud prevention.

  • Basic identity info (name, birth date, address)
  • Government ID details
  • Contact information
  • Device and behavior signals
  • Transaction patterns and risk flags (when needed)

We do not collect unrelated personal data.

Processing Lifecycle

How Your Data Is Used

Data moves through controlled, auditable steps.

Your data may be used to:

  • Train and validate KYC models
  • Build fraud detection models
  • Improve identity risk scoring
  • Support compliance monitoring

Data may be:

  • Aggregated
  • De-identified or pseudonymized
  • Used for statistics and model training

We do not sell personal data.

  1. Consent and Purpose
    We explain what we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.
  2. Secure Intake
    Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to authorized staff.
  3. Minimize and Validate
    Only required fields are kept, with quality checks before analysis or training.
  4. Train and Monitor
    We test for quality, fairness, and measurable fraud-detection gains.
  5. Retain and Dispose
    Data is kept only as long as needed, then deleted or anonymized.

Security Controls

How We Protect Data

We use technical and governance safeguards across systems and teams.

Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access controls with MFA
  • Continuous logging and alerting
  • Segregated environments and patching

Governance Safeguards

  • Purpose limitation and data minimization
  • Vendor controls and privacy training
  • Documented incident response process
  • Retention reviews and secure deletion

What We Do Not Do

  • Sell ID or selfie data
  • Use data for unrelated marketing profiling
  • Allow broad internal access to raw personal records

Breach Response

If unauthorized access is suspected, we isolate systems, investigate, and notify affected parties and regulators as required by law.
